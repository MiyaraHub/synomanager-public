Comparison

Beyond DS finder, in one app

DS finder is Synology's own app for setting up a NAS and keeping an eye on it. Syno Manager is an independent app built for the day after setup: checking on everything the NAS runs, and acting on it, without switching between apps.

Side by side

Two apps, two jobs

This is not a scorecard. DS finder does things Syno Manager does not, such as setting up a new NAS and receiving notifications from DSM's own Push Service. The table shows where each one fits.

What Syno Manager adds

The whole NAS on one screen

A health badge that names the cause

Which drive, which volume, what temperature, not just "Warning".

Control, not only status

Containers, packages, backups, services, tasks, downloads and fan mode, from the phone.

Works away from home

Photos, camera live view and file downloads over QuickConnect. No port forwarding.

Widgets and shortcuts

Twelve home screen widgets, synomanager:// links, and Siri and Shortcuts on the Mac.

An on-device assistant

Ask about your NAS in plain words. The model runs on your device and only ever reads.

Phone, tablet and desktop

Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows. $9.99 once per platform, no subscriptions.

Try it next to DS finder

A one-time $9.99 purchase. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases. Needs a Synology NAS on DSM 7.0 or later.
